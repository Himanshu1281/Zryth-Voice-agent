Spice Route Restaurant, Indore

About us: Spice Route is a pure vegetarian family restaurant in Vijay Nagar, Indore, serving North Indian, Chinese and Indori street food.

Popular dishes: Indori poha, dal bafla, paneer tikka, veg biryani, Manchurian, and our special Spice Route thali (Rs 349).

Timings: open every day, 11:30 am to 11 pm. Kitchen closes at 10:30 pm.

Table reservation: reserve a table by phone. Families and groups up to 40 people. For birthday and kitty parties, the private party hall seats 60 people.

Home delivery: on Zomato and Swiggy only.

Address: 12, Scheme No. 54, Vijay Nagar, near C21 Mall, Indore, Madhya Pradesh 452010.
