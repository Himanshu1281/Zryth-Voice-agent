Smile Care Dental Clinic, Pune

About us: Smile Care is a family dental clinic in Kothrud, Pune, running since 2009. Our team of four dentists treats children and adults.

Treatments: dental checkup and cleaning, tooth-coloured fillings, root canal treatment (RCT), dental implants, braces and clear aligners, teeth whitening, wisdom tooth removal, kids dentistry.

Doctors: Dr. Anjali Kulkarni (BDS, MDS, orthodontist), Dr. Rahul Deshpande (implants and root canal), Dr. Sneha Patil (kids dentistry), Dr. Omkar Joshi (general dentistry).

Fees: consultation and checkup Rs 500. Scaling and cleaning Rs 1,200. Root canal treatment starts at Rs 4,500. Implant and braces cost is decided after the checkup.

Timings: Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 8 pm. Sunday closed. Emergency patients are seen on the same day between 10 am and 8 pm.

Address: Shop 4, Mayur Colony, Near Karishma Society, Kothrud, Pune 411038.

Booking: patients can book an appointment by phone. We call back to confirm the slot. Payment by cash, UPI and cards. We do not accept health insurance cashless.
